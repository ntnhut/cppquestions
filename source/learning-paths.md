to study next and which prerequisites to review.

## Planned courses

The first courses will be text-based courses on **Leanpub**, with a clear lesson
sequence, annotated examples, exercises, worked solutions, and checkpoints.
**Udemy video courses** will come later, adding demonstrations and guided coding.

No courses are available yet. The first course topic will be selected using
learner feedback. All questions and their complete explanations will remain free.
